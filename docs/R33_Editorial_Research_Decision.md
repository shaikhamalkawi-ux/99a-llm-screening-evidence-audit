# 99a R33 — Expanded comprehensive-direction evidence synthesis (10 November 2026)

## What actually changed

The previous 21-example critical review was expanded into a **full project-source documentary atlas** of all 54 source-checked reports within the inherited 60-record candidate register (50 provisional empirical; four contextual). The 49 original R20 timing-note labels were preserved, and each of the five final-publisher PDFs obtained in R28–R30 was mapped to a source-specific measurement channel. The 54-row CSV includes source DOI/URL, report identity, provisional role, time/decision/reference channel, extracted source-note content and explicit claim limitations. The selected 21 close-reading examples remain the most deeply interpreted cases, not the sole evidence base.

An additional **targeted 10 November 2026 web literature search** identified **11 bibliographic works** not in the frozen 60-ID candidate register: five original LLM screening evaluations, one original multi-task assessment, three syntheses, one non-LLM comparator, and one general human-evaluation methodology review. Their titles, DOI links, source-level visibility, and provisional role are logged, and search strings and dates are deposited. None was automatically promoted to a finally eligible full-text study or pooled effect.

The main manuscript (R33) has a revised 189-word abstract, two new scientific methods/results sections, broader comparisons with 2026 prior art (including Barragán 174 AI studies and Xie 18 original evaluations), a full 54-row appendix atlas and an 11-row discovery appendix. Source scripts and database files are preserved. No author metadata or third-party copyrighted publisher PDF was fabricated or redistributed.

## Source-layer reconciliation (not a PRISMA result)

- 60 historical candidate IDs = 54 accessible source reports + four provisional scope exclusions + one abstract-only record + one duplicate-linked report.
- The 54 source reports consist of 49 inherited R20 source notes + five later publisher-PDF source audits.
- The 49 frozen R20 source-note categories are: one observed assisted vs unassisted human task; one human abstract-representation task; one human-only workflow; three human-vs-machine runtime; two calendar-vs-machine runtime; **41 did not document a matched contrast in the R20 note**. A missing contrast in an old note is **not** proof the full publisher article never reported it.
- Five additional reports give records/machine performance/modelled labor/simulated intervention cases, not complete matched assisted net staff-hour measurements.
- The 21-case purpose-built close-reading core (19 inside the 60 register and two outside it) is neither added to 54 nor a systematic inclusion total.
- The eleven update records are an additional discovery layer; they were not all completely re-extracted.

## Independent-first-reader editorial decision

**Major Revision still applies** if the manuscript is submitted as an exhaustive systematic review or as a PRISMA-ScR scoping review. The expanded map supplies a substantive broad analysis of the available source files, but it cannot retroactively satisfy database-export completeness, deduplication totals, an independently adjudicated eligibility flow or new full-text extraction for eleven discoveries. Do not report an invented PRISMA diagram. It may be defensible as an *expanded, transparent, source-traceable critical evidence map* after a journal/editor confirms the article type, and after remaining research design questions are explicitly accepted.

**Novelty guard:** Barragán et al. (2026), DOI 10.1002/cesm.70098, already inventory competing definitions of workload and time; Nunnery et al. conceptualize workflow-specific estimands; Lai discusses verification-adjusted workload; Madeyski provides decision-cost-aware reporting advice. 99a's actual additional contribution is individual *case-audit certificates* (e.g., Insuk source denominator arithmetic; Janoudi's simulated versus executed human oversight; Cao's assumed labor; Li's record filtering at recall below one), tied to source IDs and linkable verified records. These are conditional source checks, not an intervention study or a new universally validated measure.

## What is still not scientifically established

- Exhaustive 2023–2026 literature discovery across all databases (only targeted web-indexed search and inherited source material were accessible here).
- Final inclusion decisions of all 60 inherited candidates and the newly found records.
- A measured cross-paper aggregate effect on net human person-hours.
- Effect of publication overlap/cohort reuse on any hypothetical pooled result.
- Prospective independent re-coding of original studies.

There is no requirement to perform a field study to continue. The next research-stage improvement is a documented bibliographic database export and full-text source verification of the new original reports, if the intended article type is a comprehensive/systematic review; editorial or author metadata remain secondary.
