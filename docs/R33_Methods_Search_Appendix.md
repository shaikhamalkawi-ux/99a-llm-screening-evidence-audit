# R33 Methods and Source Search Transparency

**Research date:** 2026-11-10 (Asia/Dubai). **Topic:** LLM-assisted study screening; distinction between decision-volume workload, observed human labor, assumed labor rates, simulated correction, machine processing latency, and evidence/reference retention.

**Archived evidence layer:** 60 historical candidate IDs. The reproducible CSV `R33_All54_SourceNote_EvidenceAtlas.csv` links all 54 source-checked reports. `R20` frozen source notes remain unchanged; the five final-PDF overlays are separately attributed to their original rounds (R28, R29, R30). The R32 purposive 21-case sample is nested/overlapping, not appended as a new study set.

**New discovery method:** Targeted web-indexed research queries (exact searches, dates, interface, and limits: `R33_Reproducible_Web_Search_Log.csv`) and publisher or PubMed page verification. Web search surfaced publications through 2026-11-10. The actual search result denominators, complete databases and record export are not provided by this interface. An eleven-record discovery register has explicit evidence levels (`PUBLISHER_ABSTRACT...`, `PUBMED_ABSTRACT_ONLY`, `FULL_PUBLISHER_HTML_INDEXED`) and a record role. No assumptions are made about full text availability from an abstract or indexed HTML page alone.

**Novelty cross-check:** DOI `10.1002/cesm.70098` is a broad scoping review of 174 AI screening studies; its inventory already describes competing workload/time metrics. DOI `10.1111/jebm.70166` is an 18-study screening-accuracy meta-analysis. Those review denominators and the R33 54/11 evidence layers are *not additive* due to differing periods, topic and eligibility.

**Evidence class:** The timing-note categories for the first 49 records are inherited source-note fields and not freshly recoded from every original paper. For five final publisher papers, explicit scientific checks are documented. For eleven newly found publications, only accessible public-source text was used at the documented level. The evidentiary strength of any conclusion must not exceed that source level.

**No invented observations:** All new numerical values in R33 are metadata counts or direct published study results with DOI provenance. No net human savings, no novel standard-error models, no PRISMA flow, and no journal-ready comprehensive review process are claimed.
