# First-reader editorial decision — Project 99a R34

**Decision: MAJOR REVISION if submitted as a conventional comprehensive Review to MDPI Publications; not a submission approval.**

The R34 angle is substantively different from the previous source inventory. Instead of adding a new headcount of citations, it tests the strength of source-linked evidence claims under explicit arithmetic and reference-denominator constraints. Five final journal reports provide conditional counterexamples and reversal conditions, with an executable audit and negative controls. Stronger claims about the original papers or about staff-hours have not been made.

**What improves:** (a) a pointed, falsifiable question ('does this stronger claim follow?') rather than a generic warning about heterogeneous metrics; (b) an original executable *conditional* integer-feasibility illustration for Li; (c) a source-labeled full-text-coverage ceiling for a hypothetical Cao policy, with the inappropriate product of coverage and weighted sensitivity expressly rejected; (d) a transparent separation of Janoudi's executed classification comparison from hypothetical perfect human correction; (e) cross-field source arithmetic checks for Zhang/Insuk; (f) explicit conditions for reversing each audit conclusion. The 54-source atlas and 11 recent discovery records remain context, not new full eligibility results.

**Independent scientific boundaries:** this is an after-the-fact five-report choice; there is no blind independent recoding, comprehensive database-export deduplication, final review inclusion adjudication, or measured net review labor. Broad scoping reviews (Barragán 2026, 174 AI-screening studies) and methodological work (Nunnery 2026, Lai 2026, Madeyski 2026) already address metric definitions, workflow-induced estimands, verification adjustment and loss-sensitive evaluation. R34 is a compact *applied source-view audit* rather than a newly invented framework or systematic-review effect estimate.

**Editorial pivot:** R34 is better framed as a **methodological critical evidence audit / source-view case reanalysis** than as a comprehensive literature catalog. The fact that MDPI classifies Reviews as comprehensive is a real fit risk; adding more minimally checked records does not solve it. The author can consider a short, focused Article/Technical Note at a venue explicitly receptive to source-audit reanalyses, or do a genuinely exhaustive multi-database systematic protocol if the Review label must remain. No venue or article type is asserted to have approved the manuscript.

**Non-negotiable gates before journal submission:** decide a suitable article type and venue; ensure all external prior-art claims are current and grounded; reconcile article title/abstract with true scope; author metadata, conflicts and disclosure approval; rights/license; editorial check for clear distinction between conditional reconstructed integers and reported experiment data. Nothing in R34 justifies a fabricated PRISMA flow, 54/60 included count, or a 94–98% staff-time saving.

**Method:** this assessment is based on the user's R33 manuscript, 54-row document atlas, DOI-linked source display evidence, available final article PDFs, the R34 source-entered input/analysis code, and relevant published methodology as of 10 Nov 2026. The automated tests check arithmetic, logic and file integrity, not original research veracity.
