# R37 independent first-reader editorial assessment

**Decision: MAJOR REVISION — not yet journal submission ready.** This review covers the R37 source-only extension over R36, without assuming R36 is already an accepted methodological paper.

### Scientific contribution

The substantive addition is an **inverse printed-metric compatibility certificate across two distinct final-publisher case studies**, not a new metric or reviewer study. Conditional on Janoudi's fixed 3796-record Include-only policy grids and one-decimal nearest rounding, the source's printed 81.4% and 89.9% simulated precisions each correspond to *only* the full-correction/no-new-errors corner. By contrast, conditional on Li's one shared 6331-record reference and two-decimal rounding, its displayed precision 0.34, recall 0.77 and NPV 1.00 admit exactly seven count matrices, all with 26 false negatives. This strengthens the paper by distinguishing source-identifiability from metric-display ambiguity rather than adding generic robustness rhetoric. Neither contrast measures executed human errors or staff time.

### Main reviewer risks and required discipline

- **Article novelty remains the principal risk:** the mathematics is elementary, there is no new independent training or intervention, and five publisher reports were selected post hoc from an incomplete field register. The R37 contrast is a worked source-constrained illustration, not a general theory or validated claim certification standard.
- **Evidence assembly is not systematic:** 60 historical candidate IDs, 54 checked-source notes, 21 purposive close reads and 11 new bibliographic discoveries are different overlapping registers, not a PRISMA inclusion denominator. Do not claim a complete systematic/scoping review or field-wide percentages.
- **Li assumption particularly fragile:** a source-specific group of metrics can be separately averaged or constructed on a filtered subset; the seven matrices remain explicitly conditional pending original row-level outputs.
- **Human review remains hypothetical in Janoudi:** two printed precision endpoints were simulated under perfect correction. The uniqueness result is about feasible numerical states **if** the source's counts and rounding define the entire population; it is not evidence that humans actually attain those numbers.
- **Publication-type fit:** MDPI's Article type expects a substantial amount of original information; a concise Technical Note or alternative source-audit article may be better aligned, subject to the target journal's actual type availability and editor approval. Do not relabel without checking journal policy. The full-length Article candidate remains a high-risk editorial proposition.
- **Submission author gate:** names/order, correspondence, affiliations, author approval, CRediT, funding, conflict and AI-use disclosures, source rights, final data/code license and a journal type decision remain author-controlled. No emails sent to a journal.

### Exact scope of edits

R37 changes only the manuscript argument on printed metric compatibility, adds a formal Appendix K, and adds an executable exact-Fraction audit with an independent source-display input ledger. It does **not** modify original source numbers, the previously admitted research articles, the R36 fixed-policy enumeration, the study-register counts, source rankings, performance claims or final conclusions about observed staff time. Preserve immutable R36 and all earlier source snapshots.
