# Project 99a — R40 claim admission decision (11 November 2026)

**Baseline:** R39 editorial manuscript and Supplementary Appendices A–L, frozen and byte-preserved as R39 source snapshots. R40 is an author-directed interpretation-only integration: sections about source-specific routing saturation, abstention vs discrimination, and partial identification of criterion evidence, plus Appendix M and six well-established/source-data references. R40 must not be read as a new mathematical theorem or a calibrated fuzzy classifier.

| Evidence or claim | Decision | Exact boundary |
|---|---|---|
| `N=H+P+E` and 95% automatic exclusion saturation | KEEP | Follows exactly from the publicly disclosed B0/B1 routing code; not a generic property of selective classifiers. |
| SYNERGY 8 held-out reviews, 30,783 records, 315 final positives; Chan external 22, 138,902 and 598 | KEEP | Counts refer to analysis sets after documented source cleaning, not original dataset totals. |
| 90%-budget B0/B1 final-positive auto-exclusions 0/2 in SYNERGY, 4/6 in Chan | KEEP | Matched human and provisional-include counts; descriptive under final-inclusion reference, not title/abstract sensitivity or observed reviewer actions. |
| 50%-budget 67/49 SYNERGY and 74/81 Chan | KEEP | Demonstrates ranking-order dependence and lack of uniform B1 dominance. |
| 95% auto FN = 0 for all policies | QUALIFY | Because `E=0`; cannot claim model discrimination, high safety, or verified retention by humans. |
| 1,680 policy rows, 840 pairs, 480 zero-exclusion cells, 184 budget overruns | KEEP / QUALIFY | Repeated budget and synthetic routing-mask settings across only 30 holdout reviews; scenarios are dependent and do not measure labor. |
| Post-scoring abstract mask robustness | QUALIFY | Routing-only perturbation after feature/scoring, not new model inference on incomplete input text. |
| Binary final labels identify per-criterion fuzzy grades | REMOVE | An excluded record admits many latent criterion patterns; degrees cannot be identified without independently adjudicated criterion evidence/assumptions. |
| Fuzzy B2 beat/lost to B0/B1 | HOLD | B2 remained NOT_CALIBRATED, never empirically scored. |
| Causal AI benefit, staff minutes, universal screening safety or literature-wide prevalence | REMOVE | No prospective human routing outcomes, suitable randomization, person-time ledger or representative review sample. |
