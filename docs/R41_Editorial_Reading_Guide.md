# R41 — Editorial reading guide (for coauthors)

**One question:** Does a high screening-workload-reduction percentage establish fewer human hours **and** adequate retention of eligible evidence?

**Opening:** A review team can screen fewer records without having logged fewer human minutes. An apparently strong precision gain can also coincide with lower retention. We therefore trace the reported metric to its decision task, denominator, reference standard, and unresolved human verification.

**Evidence path in the article:**

1. **Human time versus routing counts.** Some source studies time people on a defined screening task; others estimate person-hours or report records not manually screened. These are not the same measurement.
2. **Five inspected publisher reports.** Source-level denominator, rounding and reference checks clarify what stronger interpretations require. Their mathematics is conditional on disclosed source assumptions.
3. **Two-channel human-correction stress.** Published simulation results require perfect correction. The exact counterfactual shows that precision can rise as recall falls if valid Includes are wrongly overturned. It does not give a human-error probability.
4. **Recorded human decisions.** The already published PICOS dataset has 1,003 citations, four reviewers and 25 source-defined positive records. Seventeen reference discordances fall on 15 records. A post hoc intersection increases precision to 100% but reduces recall to 88% or 92%; the two-stage decisions were NOT actually executed.
5. **Routing-capacity caution.** A separate external held-out B0/B1 routing check shows that at 95% nominal human budget there were no automatic exclusions because all records were either routed for human review or provisionally included. Fuzzy B2 is not calibrated and is not tested.

**What this is not:** Not a complete PRISMA systematic review; not a randomized human-assistance effect; not net person-time saved; not a validation of a new fuzzy/three-way method; not 840 independent experiments. Keep the Results as reported and the Supplement unchanged.

**Publication gate:** Author metadata and publisher-specific submission requirements still require confirmation. This note is not an editor letter and is not included in the clean journal upload by default.
