# Research-paper source access and redistribution rights

This repository intentionally **does not** mirror third-party journal-article PDFs. Publisher rights differ by article; the user-supplied full-text copies and private source audits are not blanket redistribution permissions. Use the existing source-gate DOI lists, `data/R51_2021_2026_Targeted_Citation_Register.csv`, and `reproducibility/r48/source_manifest.json` to retrieve original research from its authorized host.

For records used to analyze PICOS and crowdworker screening, the exact source releases must be retrieved separately according to their stated licenses, hashes and version. Derived quantities in this repository are not an assertion that all excluded studies were exhaustively independently adjudicated.

The project's R56 EP1 manuscript and supplement are author-review materials. They are not deposited here as a public preprint because final author approval and declarations have not been recorded. No journal acceptance or DOI is claimed.
